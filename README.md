# InnovativeWirelessPlatformsForIoT
This study investigates deep learning techniques
employed to estimate Evapotranspiration (ET ), the process by
which water is transferred from the soil and plants to the
atmosphere through evaporation or transpiration. Performing
accurate estimations of evapotranspiration is crucial in agri-
culture, where it can help to optimize irrigation and reduce
water waste. This research reviews 159 papers according to
prediction algorithms, performance metrics, datasets, and study
areas, highlighting that tree based machine learning algorithms
as Random Forest (RF) and temporal neural networks (RNN,
LSTM) are the prediction instruments most frequently used,
RMSE, R and R2 are the most used performance metrics, ground
based datasets as FLUXNET are the most widely implemented,
and Asian countries, mainly China and India, emerge as the
most frequently studied. Additionally, a personal implementation
of a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model was developed
and tested to perform temporal predictions of the actual evapo-
transpiration (AET ) in the Piedmont region in Italy. Moreover,
the performances of the model were compared to other tests to
understand its capabilities in cases where some climatological
values are missing, and the transferability to generalize over
different areas.
